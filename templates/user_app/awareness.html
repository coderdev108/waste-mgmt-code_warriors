Segregate Your Waste

Always separate dry waste (paper, plastic, metal) from wet waste (food scraps). This makes recycling easier and reduces landfill burden.

🌱
Compost Kitchen Waste

Turn food scraps, vegetable peels, and coffee grounds into rich compost. It reduces methane from landfills and creates natural fertilizer.

🛍️
Say No to Single-Use Plastic

Carry reusable bags, bottles, and containers. Single-use plastics take 400+ years to decompose and harm marine life.

📱
Proper E-Waste Disposal

Never throw electronics in regular bins. Take them to authorized e-waste collection centers to recover valuable metals safely.

💧
Reduce Water Waste

Fix leaking taps, take shorter showers, and collect rainwater. Water conservation starts at home!

🌍
